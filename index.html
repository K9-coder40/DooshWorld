About DooshWorld

The Ultimate Destination for Sound, Energy, and Unforgettable Live Experiences

Welcome to DooshWorld—a premier entertainment hub and digital stage created to elevate how music is experienced, shared, and delivered. Founded and powered by DJ Doosh, DooshWorld bridges the gap between high-octane live performances, curated sound archives, and professional audio engineering. Whether it’s turning an event into an unforgettably vibrant experience or delivering seamless studio mixtapes, DooshWorld is dedicated to the art of rhythm, precision, and crowd energy.

F.A.Q

Frequently Asked Questions About DooshWorld

How early should I book DJ Doosh for an event?

It is recommended to book at least 2 to 4 weeks in advance for weddings, club residencies, and major weekend gigs to guarantee availability. Last-minute bookings are also accepted depending on schedule availability.

What music genres are available in live sets and mixtapes?

DooshWorld specializes in multi-genre curation including Afrobeats, Amapiano, Hip-Hop, Gengetone, Reggae/Dancehall, and House music. Playlists and sets are tailored specifically to match your target audience and event theme.

Do I need to supply sound equipment and controllers?

DJ Doosh comes equipped with professional DJ controllers and performance gear. If your event venue lacks a PA system or proper sound routing, DooshWorld provides full live sound and PA equipment setup as an add-on service.

How do I order custom DJ drops and studio voice tags?

You can request custom voice tags, intro stings, and sound drops through our Audio Lab service or contact form. Submit your script and style requirements, and delivery is made within 24 to 48 hours.

What is the booking deposit and payment policy?

A 50% deposit is required upon booking confirmation to secure your date. The remaining 50% balance is settled on or before the event date prior to the performance setup.
